# What is Threadlight?

## The Big Problem

The big websites where people talk to each other have a secret: they make money by keeping you upset. Angry people click more. Scared people scroll more. Divided people argue more. And when people argue, the owners of those websites get richer. They show you things that make you mad on purpose, because it is good for their pockets.

This is bad for all of us. It makes us hate each other. It makes us forget that we are all working people with the same problems. It spreads lies. It helps bad people who want to take power and hurt others. It fills the world with robots that pretend to be people, just to trick us and tear us apart.

The big websites do not care if fascists win. They do not care if the planet burns. They only care about the numbers going up. And the easiest way to make numbers go up is to make us fight.

## What Threadlight Tries To Do

Threadlight is a different kind of place. We want to build something that is so fun and so good that people choose it over the angry websites. Not because we trick you, but because being here actually feels better.

We start from a simple idea: **the people who make the website and the people who use the website should want the same thing.** Right now, the big websites want you to be angry so they can sell your attention. We want you to be happy so we can all build something together.

## How We Get There

**We do not feed the outrage machine.** If something makes people fight, we ask why. If something spreads hate, we take it down. We do not let algorithms decide that your anger is more valuable than your peace.

**We help you find real people.** We build tools that make it hard for robots to pretend to be human. Not by asking you to find all the traffic lights in a picture, but by making it cost too much for the bad guys to bother. Real people build real communities. Robots only break them.

**We remind you that you are not alone.** Your problems are the same as your neighbor's problems. Your neighbor's problems are the same as your problems. The people who want you to fight your neighbor are the same people who want both of you to have less. When we remember this, we win.

**We make the good thing the easy thing.** If we want people to be kind, we make kindness fun. If we want people to share, we make sharing feel good. We do not scold you into being better. We build a place where being better is what everyone does.

## What We Believe

We believe that people are good when they have what they need. We believe that the people who do the work should decide how things are run. We believe that a website belongs to the people who use it and build it, not to a boss in a faraway office.

We believe that fascism grows when people are lonely, scared, and divided. So we build community. We build safety. We build solidarity.

We believe that the internet can be a place where working people from everywhere talk, share, and help each other. Not a place where we are sold to the highest bidder.

We believe that another world is possible. And we are building it, one piece at a time.

## What We Are Not

We are not neutral. When someone is being hurt and someone else is doing the hurting, we stand with the person being hurt. There is no both-sides when one side is a boot and the other side is a neck.

We are not for everyone. If you want to spread hate, if you want to trick people, if you want to make the world worse, this place is not for you. And that is okay.

We are not perfect. We will make mistakes. But we will listen, we will learn, and we will keep trying.

## Join Us

If you are tired of being fed anger, come build with us. If you want to talk to real people about real things, come sit with us. If you believe that we are stronger together than we are divided, you are already one of us.

The light is dim, but it is growing. And it threads through all of us.

**Threadlight.**
