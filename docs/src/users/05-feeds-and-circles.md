# Feeds, Circles, and the Threadlight Way

Threadlight is built to show you what matters, not what makes you mad. Most social networks use a hidden algorithm that learns one thing really well: anger keeps you scrolling. When you are angry, you stay longer, and when you stay longer, you see more ads. Threadlight does not have ads. It does not need anything from you except your presence and your care for the people around you. So its feed system works very differently.

The system that decides what you see is called the Positive Algorithm. It does not look for outrage or drama. It looks for substance and connection. The more people trust a person, the higher their posts appear. The more people find a post insightful or interesting, the more it gets shown. Posts that get marked as misleading or trolling drift downward, and people who get flagged over and over find their voice carries less weight. There is a careful balance between fresh posts and quality ones so your feed never feels stale or flooded. Every part of this process is open and transparent, and you can see exactly how it works for any post in your feed.

Next to every post there is a button that says Why This Post. Click it and Threadlight tells you plainly why that post appeared in your feed. Maybe the person is someone you follow. Maybe the post is trending in a circle you belong to. Maybe people you trust have been reacting to it warmly. There is no secret sauce, no black box, no hidden agenda. The algorithm belongs to you and your community, not to a company trying to sell you something.

Posts that attract a lot of angry or bad-faith reactions are quietly shown to fewer and fewer people. The system prefers conversation that makes people think over conversation that makes people fight. It is not about silencing anyone. It is about making sure the loudest voices are the thoughtful ones, not the cruel ones.

You can also build your own feeds. Custom feeds let you mix together posts from specific communities, posts with certain tags, posts from particular people, or any combination you like. Each custom feed can be shared outside Threadlight too, because every single one comes with an RSS feed you can drop into any news reader or email program. You build the window into the world that feels right for you.

Trending on Threadlight means something different than it does elsewhere. Instead of counting how many times a hashtag got repeated, the system actually reads what people are posting about. It looks at the text of posts that are getting attention and figures out what themes are emerging. And trending is always scoped to your communities and your circles. You see what is buzzing among the people you actually care about, not what the whole world is shouting about.

When you need to find something specific, the search tool is there for you. You can look for posts, people, communities, and tags. You can narrow things down in powerful ways, combining words and filtering by who wrote something, where it was posted, what tags it carries, or when it was written. You can even save a search you use a lot so you can come back to it quickly without typing everything again.

Circles are Threadlight's way of helping you discover people nearby who share your interests. The system looks at the topics you follow and where you are and quietly suggests groups of people you might enjoy meeting. Circles are not like communities that you have to join and keep up with. You just browse through, find someone interesting, follow them, and move on. Your privacy is protected the whole time. You can find people nearby without ever telling anyone your exact address.

Behind the scenes there is also a shared blocklist that the server keeps. It is a collection of domains and internet addresses that have proven to be a problem. It does not do anything you would notice day to day, but it works quietly in the background like a good neighbor keeping an eye on the street. Other server administrators can export this list and use it too, because safety is something we build together, not alone.
