# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Threadlight free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paid features. The server costs money to run; if you find it
useful and can afford to, you can donate. Donating gives you no special treatment.

## How is this different from Twitter, Reddit, or Mastodon?

**Twitter/X** optimizes for outrage because anger drives engagement. Threadlight's Positive
Algorithm optimizes for substance and connection. Every post has a "Why this post?" button
so you can see exactly why it was shown to you.

**Reddit** gives individual moderators unchecked power. Threadlight's M1 + M2 system
distributes moderation and holds moderators accountable through meta-moderation.

**Mastodon** is decentralized across many servers with different rules. Threadlight is one
server with one set of rules and all features in one place.

## How do trust levels work?

You progress from Level 0 (New) to Level 4 (Elder) by participating constructively over
time. Each level unlocks new abilities like flagging content, tagging posts, vouching for
users, and moderating.

## What is the Web of Trust?

The Web of Trust is a reputation graph. At Leader level, you can vouch for other users,
staking your own reputation on theirs. This prevents bot networks and makes trust meaningful.

## How does moderation work?

**M1** lets trusted users label posts as "Insightful" or "Misleading," affecting their
visibility. **M2** reviews moderators' decisions for fairness. Moderators who make
consistently unfair decisions lose their privileges. Administrators have additional tools
for serious cases. All admin actions are logged in a public moderation log.

## What kinds of reactions are there?

Beyond a single "like," you can react with: **Insightful**, **Helpful**, **Beautiful**,
**Funny**, **Wholesome**, or **Needs Sources**. Each gives different signals to the feed
algorithm.

## Can I save or organize posts?

Yes. **Collections** let you group posts with four visibility levels: private, public read,
public edit, or anybody edit. Use them for reading lists, topic trackers, or shared
collaborative curation.

## Can I schedule posts?

Yes. When creating a post, you can set a future publication time. The post is saved as a
draft and published automatically.

## What are content warnings?

Content warnings blur or hide sensitive material behind a click-through. You can add them
to any post.

## How do I find interesting content?

Browse **trending topics** (detected from post content, not hashtags), search with boolean
operators and fielded search, or explore **auto-generated circles** of people near you who
share your interests.

## What are circles?

Circles are auto-generated groups of nearby people with overlapping interests. They use
one-way location hashing so you never reveal your exact address. Browse, find interesting
people, follow them — no commitment required.

## Can I use an RSS reader?

Yes. Every feed, tag, community, and collection has an RSS/Atom export. Subscribe in any
RSS reader.

## What does "anti-fascist" mean?

Threadlight opposes the pattern of dividing people into enemies so the people at the top
can stay there. When a society is broken into warring groups, the people who own everything
keep owning everything while the rest fight each other.

This can come from any direction. On the right: nativist scapegoating and strongman worship.
On the left: cancel culture and moral enforcement. Both tell you your problems are caused
by bad people rather than by a system that concentrates wealth and power.

The **horseshoe theory** describes this pattern. Threadlight rejects it in all its forms.

## Is my data private?

We collect only what is needed to run the service. No data is sold. No cross-site tracking.
Your email is never shared unless you choose to make it visible.
