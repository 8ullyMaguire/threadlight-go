# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Polaris free?

Yes. Polaris is free to use and there are no ads. The server costs money to run (for electricity, internet, and hardware), so if you find it useful and can afford it, you can donate. But donating doesn't get you any special treatment &mdash; everyone is equal here.

## How is this different from Twitter, Reddit, or Mastodon?

**Twitter/X** is run by an algorithm designed to make you angry so you stay on the site longer. Polaris doesn't do that &mdash; you control what you see.

**Reddit** has communities (subreddits) that are controlled by a single moderator who can remove posts or ban people for any reason. Polaris uses juries so no single person has that power.

**Mastodon** is decentralized, which can be confusing. There are many different Mastodon servers, each with its own rules, and you can't always see content from other servers easily. Polaris is one server with one set of rules and all the features in one place.

## Can I lose my trust score?

Yes, but only if people stop trusting you. Trust is earned by being a decent, thoughtful person. If you're consistently rude, dishonest, or toxic, people will untrust you and your score will go down. This isn't a punishment from an admin &mdash; it's just the community's honest opinion of you.

## What happens if I break the rules?

Someone can report your post or behavior. A jury of random users will review the case. You get to explain your side. The jury votes on what should happen: a warning, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban. Nobody gets banned without a fair review by their peers.

## Who runs this place?

The server is run by whoever hosts it. But the rules and moderation are handled collectively by the community through juries. The person running the server can't just ban you because they disagree with you &mdash; everything goes through the jury system.

## Can I create my own community?

Absolutely! Anyone can create a community. New communities start in "slow boot" mode, which means they grow slowly at first. Once your community has proven it can be healthy and constructive, it can be promoted out of slow boot.

## What does "anti-fascist" mean?

It means we oppose the pattern of dividing people into enemies so the people at the top can
stay there. When a society is broken into warring groups &mdash; left vs right, urban vs
rural, native vs foreigner &mdash; the people who own everything keep owning everything while
the rest of us fight each other.

This pattern can come from any direction. On the right it looks like nativist scapegoating
and strongman worship. On the left it looks like cancel culture and moral enforcement. Both
tell you your problems are caused by a group of bad people rather than by a system that
concentrates wealth and power.

The **horseshoe theory** describes this: the far left and far right bend toward each other
like the ends of a horseshoe, developing the same authoritarian habits with different
branding. Both abandon free speech. Both reduce people to group labels. Both seek to impose
their will through intimidation rather than argument. One side uses pitchforks; the other
uses social ostracism.

Polaris rejects this pattern in all its forms. We believe the people who work for a living
have more in common with each other than with the people at the top, and that the only way
to build something better is to talk to each other instead of fighting for the powerful.

## Is my data private?

We collect only the information needed to run the service (email, username, your posts). We don't sell your data, we don't track you across the web, and we don't show you targeted ads. Your email is never shared publicly unless you choose to make it visible.

## What if I see something I don't like?

You have several options: hide the post, block the person, report it for rule-breaking, or add a community note to provide context. You can also create content filters to hide posts with certain words or topics. You are in control of your experience.

## Can I donate to support the server?

If this server has a donation link, you can contribute. Your participation in the community is already valuable enough, and donations just help keep the server running.

## What does "slow boot" mean for communities?

Slow boot is a period at the beginning of a new community's life where it grows slowly. This gives the founders time to set the culture and rules before too many people join. Once the community is established and healthy, it can be upgraded out of slow boot mode.

## Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can delete your account from your settings. This removes your personal information and profile. Your posts may remain (to keep conversations intact) but they'll be anonymized.

## How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

If you find a bug or have an idea for a new feature, you can report it on the project's GitHub page at [github.com/anomalyco/polaris](https://github.com/anomalyco/polaris).

## I'm being harassed. What should I do?

First, block the person immediately. Then report any harassing posts using the report function. Don't engage with the harasser. If the harassment continues, contact a moderator. Harassment is against the rules and the jury system is there to protect you.
